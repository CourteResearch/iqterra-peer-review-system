
1. Contribution & Points Allocation Questions

These rely on TEAMMATES’ "Distribute Points" question type, which forces students to balance out a fixed pool of points among peers to prevent grade inflation.
• The "Equal Share" Question: “For this project cycle, allocate a score out of 100 to each team member representing their share of the workload (Give 100 for equal contribution, >100 for doing more, <100 for doing less).”
• Team Contribution Index: “Divide 10 points per team member (excluding yourself) based on their actual productivity and task completion.”

2. Qualitative (Open-Ended) Feedback Questions

TEAMMATES uses these "Essay" or "Text" question types to let students write constructive, actionable feedback to each other.
• The "Stop, Start, Continue" Format:
	• “What is one specific thing this teammate should stop doing to help the team function better?”
	• “What is one specific action this teammate should start doing for the next sprint?”
	• “What does this teammate do exceptionally well that they should continue doing?”
• Constructive Critique: “Which skill or work habit, if this teammate developed it further, would make the biggest difference to the team's output?”

3. Professionalism & Work-Ethic Rubrics

These are configured using TEAMMATES' "Rubric" or "Multiple Choice (Grid)" features, allowing students to rate peers on a 4 or 5-point scale (e.g., Excellent, Very Good, Satisfactory, Marginal, Unsatisfactory).
• Reliability: “How reliably did this team member attend scheduled meetings, log in on time, and communicate when they were running late?”
• Quality of Work: “Rate the quality, technical accuracy, and completeness of the individual tasks assigned to this teammate.”
• Team Interaction: “How effectively did this person listen to different viewpoints and accept constructive criticism gracefully?”

4. Self-Evaluation & Team Health Checks

TEAMMATES allows instructors to create questions where the recipient is "Self" or "The Team as a whole".
• Self-Reflection: “Give a specific example of a core concept you learned from your team members during this milestone that you would not have figured out alone.”
• Team Operations: “Overall, how effectively did your team navigate roadblocks and work together during this development cycle?”
